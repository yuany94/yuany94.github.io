Background

Before OpenAI, I was a tenure-track assistant professor at UC Davis (2024–2025) and Purdue University (2021–2024). I received my PhD from MIT’s Institute for Data, Systems, and Society, and bachelor’s degrees in Computer Science and Economics from Tsinghua University.

My previous industry research experience includes a visiting research role at Microsoft and a research internship at Facebook Core Data Science.

Earlier research

Networks, experimentation, and social behavior

Previously, I worked as an interdisciplinary researcher at the intersection of AI and empirical science, publishing in general science journals, business-school journals, and computer science conferences.

My earlier work explored social and organizational networks, causal inference and online experiments, and the use of large language models in social science.
